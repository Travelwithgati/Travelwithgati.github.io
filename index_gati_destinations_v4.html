A little history

Mussoorie grew as a hill station during the British period in the 19th century and became one of the best-known mountain retreats in North India.

Spirituality • Adventure

Rishikesh

Where the Ganga, yoga, ashrams and Himalayan adventure meet.

A little history

Rishikesh has long been associated with Hindu pilgrimage and spiritual learning. Its international profile grew strongly in the 20th century as yoga and meditation attracted visitors from around the world.

High Himalaya • Snow

Auli

A high-altitude meadow destination famous for dramatic Himalayan views and winter snow.

Why visit?

Auli sits in the Garhwal Himalaya near Joshimath and is known for its ski slopes, open meadows and views toward major Himalayan peaks.

Kumaon • Lake Town

Nainital

A beautiful lake town wrapped by wooded hills, boating and classic Kumaoni scenery.

A little history

Nainital developed as a hill station in the 19th century around Naini Lake and later became the summer seat of the colonial United Provinces government.

Heritage & history

Places with a story to tell.

Concise descriptions based on current Uttarakhand Tourism information.

Mussoorie

Mussoorie developed as a hill station in the 19th century and became a well-known Himalayan retreat, with a strong colonial-era character.

Jageshwar

Jageshwar is known for an important group of ancient Shiva temples set among deodar forests, combining heritage and pilgrimage.

Kedarnath

Kedarnath is a Char Dham site and one of Shiva's twelve Jyotirlingas, dramatically located beneath high Himalayan peaks.

Nainital

Nainital grew around its famous lake and became a prominent Himalayan hill station, with a distinctive heritage landscape.
