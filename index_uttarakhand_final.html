Heritage & history

Places with a story to tell.

Concise descriptions based on current Uttarakhand Tourism information.

Mussoorie

Mussoorie developed as a hill station in the 19th century and became a well-known Himalayan retreat, with a strong colonial-era character.

Jageshwar

Jageshwar is known for an important group of ancient Shiva temples set among deodar forests, combining heritage and pilgrimage.

Kedarnath

Kedarnath is a Char Dham site and one of Shiva's twelve Jyotirlingas, dramatically located beneath high Himalayan peaks.

Nainital

Nainital grew around its famous lake and became a prominent Himalayan hill station, with a distinctive heritage landscape.
